# FocusGo

**Make every day count.**

FocusGo is an all-in-one productivity app that helps you focus, study, and stay on top of your day — with a built-in Pomodoro-style focus timer, study/exam tracking, task management, notes, and even prayer time (Salah) and Qibla tools.

🔗 **Live app:** [focusgo.vercel.app](https://focusgo.vercel.app)

---

## ✨ Features

- ⏱️ **Focus Timer** — Pomodoro-style focus/break sessions with sound, vibration, and fullscreen focus mode
- 📚 **Study Planner** — Track subjects, chapters, and exams with progress stats and daily study plans
- ✅ **Tasks** — Create, organize, and complete daily tasks
- 📝 **Notes** — Rich text notes with formatting, color labels, and pinning
- 📅 **Calendar** — Month view with completed topics, exams, and holidays
- 🕌 **Salah Times & Qibla** — Live prayer times based on your location, Asr calculation (Hanafi/Shafi), and Qibla direction
- 🌤️ **Weather** — Local weather at a glance
- ⏰ **Alarms** — Quick alarm and reminders
- 🔥 **Streaks & Stats** — Daily progress tracking and streak counters
- 🌗 **Light / Dark mode**
- 🌐 **Bilingual** — Full English and Bengali (বাংলা) language support
- 🔐 **Google Sign-In** — Sync your data securely across devices

- **Firebase** — Authentication & Firestore (data sync)
